Why It Matters

The Desert Works Against
Your Investment Every Day

The Mojave is one of the dustiest environments in the country. Without regular cleaning, your panels quietly underperform — and you pay the price on your electric bill.

🏜️
Mojave Dust
Fine desert dust coats panels faster here than almost anywhere in the US. No rain means it never gets washed off naturally.
☀️
Peak Sun = Peak Loss
The more sunlight your area gets, the more you lose when panels are dirty. Barstow gets 300+ sunny days a year — clean panels matter more here.
🐦
Bird Droppings
Concentrated spots from bird droppings can reduce output on individual cells by up to 40% — far worse than general dust.
💧
Hard Water Spots
High desert water is mineral-heavy. DIY hosing often leaves calcium deposits that bond to glass and cut efficiency further.
